About Us

ChargeFinder (KERALA) is a platform dedicated to helping electric vehicle (EV) owners easily locate nearby charging stations in Kerala. Our goal is to simplify the EV experience by providing accurate, up-to-date information about charging locations in Kerala.

Whether you're planning a long trip or just need a quick charge nearby, ChargeFinder helps you find the best charging spot with a single click.

We are committed to supporting a greener future by making EV charging more accessible, more efficient, and more user-friendly.

Thank you for choosing ChargeFinder (KERALA). Let's power up the future together!
